# AI Video Promotion Prompts

Use these prompts in AI Video Generators (like OpenAI Sora, Runway Gen-2, Kling, or Luma Dream Machine) to create viral marketing content for **QuietSend**.

---

## 🛑 Concept 1: The "Glitch" Reveal (High Tech / Cyberpunk)
**Goal**: Show that "things are not what they seem."
**Style**: Cinematic, Dark Mode, Matrix-style green/black.

### 🎬 The Prompt:
> A close-up cinematic shot of a mundane digital photo of a cute golden retriever dog on a smartphone screen. Suddenly, the image glitches with digital artifacts and green code rain (Matrix style). The dog photo peels away like digital paper to reveal a hidden secret message underneath that says "MEET ME AT MIDNIGHT" in glowing neon red text. The lighting is dark and moody, reflection of the phone screen on a user's face. High resolution, 8k, photorealistic, cyberpunk aesthetic.

---

## 🕵️ Concept 2: The "Spy Thriller" (James Bond Vibe)
**Goal**: Make the user feel like a secret agent.
**Style**: Noir, Shadowy, Tense.

### 🎬 The Prompt:
> A spy movie scene in a rainy Tokyo alleyway at night using a neon-lit smartphone. The user's thumb presses a "Decrypt" button on a sleek black app interface. A seemingly innocent image of a sushi platter dissolves smoothly into a detailed blueprint of a building. Raindrops on the screen. Bokeh lights in background. Intense atmosphere. Text on screen changes from "Access Denied" to "Payload Extracted". 8k, cinematic lighting, movie trailer style.

---

## 🤯 Concept 3: The "Magic Trick" (Visual Optical Illusion)
**Goal**: Pure visual satisfaction.
**Style**: Clean, Minimalist, Apple-style commercial.

### 🎬 The Prompt:
> A pristine white desk setup with an iPad Pro displaying a high-resolution landscape photo of a mountain. A hand drags a digital magnifying glass over the mountain peak. As the glass passes over, the pixels of the mountain rearrange themselves into clean sans-serif text that reads "YOUR SECRETS ARE SAFE". The transition is incredibly smooth and liquid-like. Soft diffused lighting, studio quality, 4k, macro shot.

---

## 👻 Concept 4: The "Ghost" (Horror / Mystery)
**Goal**: Stop the scroll on TikTok (Scary/Mysterious works best).
**Style**: Glitch horror, VHS static.

### 🎬 The Prompt:
> A POV shot holding a phone in a dark room. The screen shows a static noise image. Slowly, a face begins to form inside the static, but turns into text instead. The text glows faintly: "THEY ARE WATCHING. USE QUIETSEND." The camera shakes slightly as if the user is nervous. VHS tape grain effect, analog horror aesthetic, unsettling but intriguing.

---

## 🎵 Recommended Audio (Add this in Post-Production):
*   **Concept 1 (Glitch)**: Heavy bass drop + digital "chirp" sounds.
*   **Concept 2 (Spy)**: Ticking clock sound + deep synth drone.
*   **Concept 3 (Magic)**: Satisfying "click" and "whoosh" sound effects. ASMR style.
*   **Concept 4 (Ghost)**: White noise + low frequency rumble.
