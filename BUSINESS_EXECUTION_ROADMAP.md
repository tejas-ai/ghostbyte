# 📅 The 30-Day "Zero to Hero" Business Roadmap
**Goal**: Go from "Just Code" to "First Dollar Earned" (₹1).

You start **TOMORROW**. Here is exactly what to do.

---

## 🏁 Week 1: Look Like a Real Business (Building Trust)
*Focus: Professionalism. People don't pay for "hobby projects".*

*   **Day 1 (Tomorrow): The Domain Name**
    *   **Task**: Buy a real domain name (e.g., `quietsend.net` or `quietsend.io`).
    *   *Where*: Namecheap or GoDaddy. Cost: ~₹800.
    *   *Why*: `quietsend.vercel.app` looks amateur. `quietsend.net` looks valuable.
*   **Day 2: The "About" Page**
    *   **Task**: Write a clean "About" page.
    *   *Content*: "We believe privacy is a human right. Built for journalists, researchers, and you."
    *   *Why*: Trust. People need to know *why* you built it.
*   **Day 3: Analytics (The Eyes)**
    *   **Task**: Add **PostHog** (Free tier) or **Google Analytics**.
    *   *Why*: You need to know if people are visiting. "You can't fix what you can't measure."

---

## 📢 Week 2: Make Noise (Traffic Generation)
*Focus: Getting your first 1,000 visitors. Use the `marketing.md` strategies.*

*   **Day 4-5: The "Glitch" Video**
    *   **Task**: Use your AI Video Prompts. Generate 3 short videos (15 seconds).
    *   **Action**: Post on TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels.
    *   *Caption*: "The file transfer tool they don't want you to know about. 🤫 #privacy"
*   **Day 6: The "Reddit" Launch**
    *   **Task**: Post on `r/SideProject` and `r/WebDev`.
    *   **Title**: "I built a Steganography tool that runs 100% in the browser (WASM). Roast my code."
    *   *Why*: "Roast my code" gets more clicks than "Please use my app".
*   **Day 7: The "GitHub" Readme**
    *   **Task**: Make your GitHub README beautiful. Add screenshots.
    *   *Why*: Developers will star it. Stars = Credibility.

---

## 💰 Week 3: The "Ask" (Monetization Setup)
*Focus: Setting up the cash register.*

*   **Day 8: The "Donation" Link**
    *   **Task**: create a **"Buy Me a Coffee"** page (buymeacoffee.com).
    *   **Action**: Add a small button in your app header: *"Support Privacy ☕"*.
    *   *Why*: It's the easiest way to earn your first ₹100.
*   **Day 9-10: The "Pro" Plan Design**
    *   **Task**: Create a pricing page (even if the payment button doesn't work yet).
    *   *Content*: "Free: 5MB limit. Pro: 50MB limit + Hide EXE."
    *   *Goal*: See if people click "Upgrade". This tests demand.

---

## 🚀 Week 4: The "Hustle" (Direct Sales)
*Focus: finding paying customers.*

*   **Day 11-14: The "Influencer" DM**
    *   **Task**: Find 10 Tech YouTubers / Twitter accounts (with <50k followers).
    *   **Message**: "Hey, I built a privacy tool that hides files inside images. It's like Mr. Robot's tech. Wrote a script for a short video if you want to use it."
    *   *Why*: If one of them posts it, you win.

---

## ⚠️ The "Golden Rule" for You
**Do NOT assume.**
*   Don't assume people want it. **Ask them.**
*   Don't code new features (Dark Mode, etc.) until you have **100 users**.
*   **Marketing > Coding**. Spend 20% time coding, 80% time posting online.

**Your Goal for Day 30**: Earn **₹1**.
Once you earn ₹1, earning ₹1 Lakh is just doing the same thing 100,000 times.
