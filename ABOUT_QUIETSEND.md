# 👻 QuietSend: The Art of Invisible Data

> *"In a world of total surveillance, the only true privacy is invisibility."*

**Professional-grade multi-modal steganography suite for the modern age. Conceal messages and files inside images and audio tracks with mathematical imperceptibility.**

## 🔍 System Overview
**QuietSend** is an advanced, client-side steganography platform designed for the secure concealment of sensitive digital assets. Unlike traditional encryption which overtly signals ciphertext existence, QuietSend conceals payloads inside the pixel matrix of digital photos and the sample bitstream of uncompressed WAV audio files.

## 🛠️ Core Modules (v3.0 PRO)

### 1. 🧬 The Multi-Modal Encoder
Fuses your **Cover Image (PNG/TIFF/WebP)** or **Cover Audio (16-bit PCM WAV)** with secret messages or multi-file archives.
* **Dual Encryption**: Choose between **Symmetric AES-GCM-256 (600,000 PBKDF2 iterations)** or **Asymmetric ECDH P-256 Public Key Cryptography**.
* **Honey-Vault Defense**: Configure dual passphrases for coercion resistance (decoy password delivers innocent files).
* **1-Click PKZIP Document Bypass**: Generates lossless archives that bypass WhatsApp, Telegram, and Signal re-compression.

### 2. 🔓 The Multi-Modal Decoder
* **Auto-Detection**: Instantly identifies LSB-4, LSB-6, and PCM audio carrier formats.
* **Keyring Integration**: 1-click decryption using local private identity keys.
* **Multi-File GhostVault Extraction**: Safely downloads all bundled files with SHA-256 verification.

### 3. 🔑 Asymmetric Keyring Studio
* Generate ECDH P-256 keypairs with 16-character hex fingerprints.
* Share armored public keys or import recipient contact keys for direct asymmetric encryption.

### 4. 🛡️ Forensic Scanner & NIST CAVP Diagnostics
* Real-time PSNR, MSE, difference heatmaps, and 8-level bit-plane slicing.
* Live cryptographic vector self-tests benchmarking encryption latency and memory safety.
